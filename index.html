Relic Pack Farming Locations

Syndicate Relic Packs

You can purchase Relic Packs from any of the 6 main Syndicates (Steel Meridian, Arbiters of Hexis, Cephalon Cuda, The Perrin Sequence, Red Veil, New Loki) for 20,000 Standing each. This is one of the most reliable ways to get new relics on release day.

Steel Path Honors (Gotiniq Wuicho)

Visit Chichaka in any Relay and browse his Steel Path Honors. You can purchase a pack of 3 random relics for 15 Steel Essence. This is a great option if you regularly play Steel Path missions.

Midata (Cetus)

Visit Durtiq Minior in Cetus. At Rank3, you can purchase Relic Packs for 20,000 Standing.

Slunchewata Zadruga (Fortuna)

Visit Dimqsht Prust in Fortuna. At Rank 2, you can purchase Relic Packs for 20,000 Standing.

Wlizashtite (Necralisk)

Visit Mozuchko in the Necralisk. At Rank 2, you can purchase Relic Packs for 20,000 Standing.
